Frequently Asked Questions

How fast will my order complete? +
Most orders are checked and processed within minutes. You can always track live status using your Order ID.
Do I need to share my Free Fire password? +
No — you only need to provide your Player UID and Server ID. Never share your account password with anyone, including support staff.
Which payment methods are supported? +
bKash, Nagad and Rocket. Payment numbers and instructions are configurable from the admin panel — always read them on the order page before paying.
What if my payment succeeds but the order stays pending? +
Use the Order Tracking page with your Order ID. Admins update status manually after verifying the transaction reference. If something is wrong, contact support.
Are wallet rewards real money? +
The wallet is an in-app balance for ordering diamonds and rewarding engagement. Real money in/out is only available once a real payment gateway is connected.
